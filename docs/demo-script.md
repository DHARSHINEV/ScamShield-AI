# ScamShield AI — Hackathon Judging Demo Script (2m 30s)

**Target Track:** Digital Safety & Cybersecurity / AI for Everyday Life  
**Theme:** Detect it. Understand it. Practice it. Prevent it.

---

### [0:00 – 0:15] Hook: The Everyday Problem
> **Presenter:**  
> "Judges, imagine waking up to this SMS:  
> *'🚨 SBI ALERT: Your account will be blocked today. Verify your KYC immediately: sbi-verify-secure.xyz'*  
> An ordinary citizen panics, clicks, and within minutes their savings are wiped out. Why? Because traditional antivirus flags an IP, but ordinary people need to know: *Is this suspicious? Why? And what should I do right now?*  
> That is why we built **ScamShield AI**."
